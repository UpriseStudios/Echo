Echo Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026 · Applies to Echo on Android and iOS

Echo is a one-tap runner where every failed run comes back as a ghost. It has no accounts and no Echo servers: your game lives on your phone. This policy explains the little data that leaves it, and why.

Who we are

Echo is made by Uprise (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the data described in this policy. You can reach us at hello@uprisestudios.co.uk.

What stays on your device

Echo saves your progress in a file on your phone: your best score and stats, your ghosts (stored as the timing of your taps, not as video), achievements, the skins and power-ups you hold, your highlights reel, your settings and privacy choices, and the name you choose for Ghost Challenges. We never receive this file. Uninstalling Echo deletes it.

  • Ghost Challenges. When you send a challenge, Echo builds a link containing your run, your chosen name and the skin you wore. It goes only to the people you send it to, through the app you pick.
  • Highlights reel clips. Clips are made on your phone and shared only when you choose to, through your phone’s share sheet.

Your privacy choice

The first time you open Echo we ask before anything starts. Accept all allows personalised ads and anonymous gameplay analytics. Essential only turns both off. Nothing from Unity or our ad partners starts until you have chosen. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy choices. On iPhone, personalised ads also need your permission in Apple’s tracking prompt.

Data handled by our partners

Rewarded ads: Unity LevelPlay

Echo only shows ads you choose to watch (to continue a run, banish a ghost, unlock a character or earn a power-up), never during a run, and never as banners or pop-ups. To load, show and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with process device data such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. If you choose Essential only, the ads you see are not personalised.

Gameplay analytics: Unity Analytics

Only if you accept analytics, Echo sends anonymous gameplay events to Unity Analytics so we can balance the game: for example when a run starts and ends, its score and distance, and when power-ups, ads, purchases or Ghost Challenges are used. Unity also records basic device information and a random installation identifier. We do not collect your name, email or contacts. If you later switch analytics off, Echo stops sending events and asks Unity to delete the analytics data it holds for your device.

Purchases: Google Play and the App Store

Skin packs are bought through Google Play or the App Store, using Unity IAP. The store handles your payment; we only receive confirmation of what was bought so we can unlock it. We never see your card or payment details.

Each partner’s own policy explains how it uses data: Unity (LevelPlay, Analytics, IAP), Google and Apple.

Why we may use it

  • Your consent for personalised ads and analytics. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings.
  • Providing the game you asked for for showing the rewarded ads you choose to watch and unlocking purchases.
  • Legitimate interests in keeping ads working and free of fraud.

How long it is kept, and security

Your save stays on your phone until you uninstall Echo. Data held by Unity, its ad partners and the app stores is kept under their own policies. Everything Echo sends over the internet is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not sell your data.

Children

Echo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used Echo and data about them has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the UK and the EU, you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data about you, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Because Echo has no accounts and keeps your game on your phone, the quickest way to remove it is to switch off analytics in Settings and uninstall the app. For anything else, email us. If you are in the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk), or in the EU to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Echo handles data, we will update this page and its effective date, and ask again in the game when a change needs your consent.
