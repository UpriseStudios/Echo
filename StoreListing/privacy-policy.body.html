Echo Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026 · Applies to Echo on Android and iOS

Echo is a one-tap runner where every failed run comes back as a ghost. Your game lives on your phone. Its online features (the Daily Haunt and challenge links, in version 2.0 and later) use an anonymous online ID and our game server, and only once you use them. This policy explains the little data that leaves your phone, and why.

Who we are

Echo is made by Uprise (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the data described in this policy. You can reach us at hello@uprisestudios.co.uk.

What stays on your device

Echo saves your progress in a file on your phone: your best score and stats, your ghosts (stored as the timing of your taps, not as video), achievements, the skins and power-ups you hold, your highlights reel, your settings and privacy choices, and the name you choose for Ghost Challenges. We never receive this file. Uninstalling Echo deletes it.

  • Ghost Challenges. When you send a challenge, your run, the ghosts it met, your chosen name and the skin you wore go into a link, either inside the link itself or kept on our game server for the link to open (see below). It goes to the people you send it to, through the app you pick.
  • Highlights reel clips. Clips are made on your phone and shared only when you choose to, through your phone’s share sheet.

Online features: the Daily Haunt and challenges

These features are in Echo version 2.0 and later; earlier versions have no online features. Nothing here happens until you open the Daily Haunt, send or open a challenge, or share a clip with a challenge link. Then:

  • An anonymous online ID. Echo signs in to Unity Authentication without any details from you: it creates a random player ID and keeps a sign-in token on your phone. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your runs, as taps. Echo sends a run as the timing of your taps and the ghosts it met, never video, location or contacts. Our server replays it with the game's own rules to check it.
  • The Daily Haunt. We keep your best run of the day and up to 20 of your other attempts (they come back as your own ghosts), deleted 7 days after that day. The daily leaderboard shows your best distance with a name Unity generates for you, such as SwiftGhost#4821; you can't set it, and it says nothing about you. The day's top 10 runs appear to other players as ghosts with that name. After midnight UTC the day's board is kept as an archive of IDs, generated names and distances.
  • Challenges. A challenge you send is kept on our server with your chosen name (names on a blocklist become "Runner") and opens for anyone with its link for 30 days, after which it is deleted.
  • Switches. Echo asks Unity Remote Config whether the online features are switched on, sending your random ID.

Deleting your online data

Settings › Delete online data deletes your Daily Haunt runs, your entry on today's board, the challenges you've sent and your online ID. Past days' archived boards can't be edited by anyone; they hold only the random ID, the generated name and a distance, which nothing links to you once your online ID is deleted. Uninstalling Echo does not delete online data, and afterwards nothing tells us which data was yours, so use the button first.

Your privacy choice

The first time you open Echo we ask before anything starts. Accept all allows personalised ads and anonymous gameplay analytics. Essential only turns both off. Nothing from Unity or our ad partners starts until you have chosen. You can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy choices. On iPhone, personalised ads also need your permission in Apple’s tracking prompt.

Data handled by our partners

Rewarded ads: Unity LevelPlay

Echo only shows ads you choose to watch (to continue a run, banish a ghost, unlock a character or earn a power-up), never during a run, and never as banners or pop-ups. To load, show and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it works with process device data such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device model and operating system, and how you interact with ads. If you choose Essential only, the ads you see are not personalised.

Gameplay analytics: Unity Analytics

Only if you accept analytics, Echo sends anonymous gameplay events to Unity Analytics so we can balance the game: for example when a run starts and ends, its score and distance, and when power-ups, ads, purchases or Ghost Challenges are used. Unity also records basic device information and a random installation identifier. We do not collect your name, email or contacts. If you later switch analytics off, Echo stops sending events and asks Unity to delete the analytics data it holds for your device. If you have used the online features, Unity Analytics may also see your random online ID.

Online features: Unity Gaming Services

The Daily Haunt and challenges run on Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Code, Cloud Save, Leaderboards and Remote Config), which store and process this data for us, as described above.

Purchases: Google Play and the App Store

Skin packs are bought through Google Play or the App Store, using Unity IAP. The store handles your payment; we only receive confirmation of what was bought so we can unlock it. We never see your card or payment details.

Each partner’s own policy explains how it uses data: Unity (LevelPlay, Analytics, IAP, Gaming Services), Google and Apple.

Why we may use it

  • Your consent for personalised ads and analytics. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings.
  • Providing the game you asked for for showing the rewarded ads you choose to watch, unlocking purchases, and running the Daily Haunt and challenges when you use them.
  • Legitimate interests in keeping ads working and free of fraud.

How long it is kept, and security

Your save stays on your phone until you uninstall Echo. Daily Haunt runs are deleted 7 days after their day and challenges after 30 days; everything online goes sooner if you use Delete online data. Data held by Unity, its ad partners and the app stores is kept under their own policies. Everything Echo sends over the internet is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not sell your data.

Children

Echo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used Echo and data about them has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the UK and the EU, you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data about you, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Because Echo has no accounts and keeps your game on your phone, the quickest way to remove your data is Settings › Delete online data, then switching off analytics, then uninstalling the app. For anything else, email us. If you are in the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk), or in the EU to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Echo handles data, we will update this page and its effective date, and ask again in the game when a change needs your consent.
