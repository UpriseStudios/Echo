# Marketing — Content Plan

## Goals, measures and setup

For the next two weeks, the goal is to find 2 or 3 clips that reliably send people to the Play Store, using organic posts only. Money goes in only after that, and only behind a clip that's already working.

**What to measure**

| Measure | Where | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Views, watch time, shares per clip | Each platform's analytics | Which hooks stop the scroll |
| Profile and link clicks | Each platform's analytics | Which clips make people curious enough to look |
| Store visits and installs by source | Play Console → Store analysis → Acquisition | Which platform actually brings players |
| Challenge links opened | Unity Analytics (`challenge_opened`) | Whether challenge posts turn into play |
| Day-1 and day-7 return rate | Play Console or Unity Analytics | Whether new players stay, before spending anything |

**Before day 1**

- [ ] Set up TikTok, YouTube (for Shorts) and Instagram accounts for Echo, all with the same name and profile picture (the Echo icon).
- [ ] Make one tagged Play Store link per platform, so Play Console shows where installs came from. For example: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.uprise.echo&referrer=utm_source%3Dtiktok%26utm_medium%3Dsocial%26utm_campaign%3Dlaunch` (swap `tiktok` for `youtube`, `instagram`, `reddit`). Put each in that platform's bio.
- [ ] Run Prompt 6 (recording mode and marketing export) with Claude Code, so clips come out clean and in the right format.
- [ ] Record two sessions of about 30 minutes each: one main game with lots of ghosts and a Nemesis, one Daily Haunt. That's enough footage for the first week.
- [ ] Note down 2.0's current day-1 and day-7 return rates as your starting point.

**Clip rules**

- Vertical 9:16, 9 to 20 seconds.
- Gameplay visible in the first second, with the hook as on-screen text in the top third.
- One idea per clip.
- Keep captions and key moments away from the bottom and right edges, where TikTok and Reels put their buttons.
- Everything shown must be in the game as players get it.
- Use sounds cleared for business use, or the game's own music.

## Content pillars and 14-day calendar

Every clip belongs to one of six pillars, so the feed has variety but stays clearly about one game.

| Pillar | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| The mechanic | Deaths turning into ghosts and the course filling up |
| Nemesis stories | One ghost that keeps killing you, its name, the duel |
| Ghost Challenges | A hard run thrown out as a challenge, then replies and rematches |
| Daily Haunt series | A daily attempt at the shared course against the top 10 |
| Dev diary | Design decisions told in a self-aware developer voice |

## Reddit, paid test and Christmas

**Reddit plan**

Lead with the design story, not the store link. Reddit rewards developers who share something interesting and stick around to talk about it.

| Date | Where | Angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sun 1 Nov | r/IndieDev | "I made every failed run an obstacle. At 6 ghosts the game was impossible, so ghosts now wait where they died." With a short GIF of before and after. |
| Sun 8 Nov | r/indiegames | "My game has a daily course where today's top 10 runs haunt you as ghosts." With a Daily Haunt GIF and a question asking what people think. |

- **Read each subreddit's rules first.** Self-promotion rules differ: r/gamedev only allows it in specific threads, and r/AndroidGaming has its own posting rules.
- **No link in the post body.** Add the store link in a comment if the subreddit allows it, or answer when someone asks.
- **Reply to every comment for the first few hours.** That's what keeps a post visible.
- **Never post the same thing to several subreddits on the same day.**

**Paid test (from about 6 November, only if a clip is clearly winning)**

- **Budget:** £50 to £100 in total, spent at £5 to £10 a day.
- **What to put money behind:** the one clip with the best ratio of profile or link clicks to views. Not the one with the most views.
- **Where:** boost that clip on the platform it won on (TikTok Promote or a Spark Ad, or an Instagram boost). Or try a small Google App campaign, which only needs your existing store assets. With a budget this small, Google's system may not have enough data to optimise, so judge it after a week rather than day by day.
- **Success means:** installs at a cost you can live with, and paid players coming back the next day at a similar rate to organic players.
- **Stop if:** paid players' day-1 return rate is much lower than organic players', or the cost per install keeps rising after 3 days.

- "30 ghosts, all dressed as Santa." The screenshot moment.

## Prompt pack: writing and review

Paste these into a Claude chat. Fill in the parts in square brackets.

**Prompt 1: Caption pack for a clip**

```
I make Echo, a free one-tap mobile runner (Google Play) where every failed run comes back as a ghost waiting just before where you died. Touch one and you're done. Features: Nemesis ghost with red horns and a name, near-miss points, bounty ghosts, Daily Haunt (shared daily course haunted by the day's top 10 runs), friend Ghost Challenges, the Reel Cupboard replay editor, 16-bit pixel art, no forced ads.

Clip: [what happens in the clip, second by second]
Pillar: [mechanic / Nemesis / challenge / Daily Haunt / dev diary / skins and worlds]

Write:
1. Three on-screen hooks, max 8 words each, readable in under 2 seconds.
2. A TikTok caption (max 150 characters) ending in a question that invites comments.
3. A YouTube Shorts title (max 60 characters).
4. An Instagram Reels caption (1 to 2 short lines).
5. Up to 4 hashtags per platform, mixing broad and specific.
Voice: a self-aware solo developer, a little self-deprecating, never corporate. Only mention features that are in the clip or the list above.
```

**Prompt 2: Fresh hooks**

```
Echo is a one-tap runner where every failed run comes back as a ghost that kills on contact. Give me 20 new hooks for short gameplay clips, for this pillar: [pillar]. Max 8 words each. Mix curiosity ("this ghost has killed me 9 times"), challenge ("bet you can't pass 10 of these"), and confession ("I made my own game too hard"). No clickbait that the clip can't pay off. Mark your 3 strongest.
```

**Prompt 3: Reddit post**

```
Draft a Reddit post for [subreddit] about a design decision in my game Echo: [the decision and what it fixed]. Lead with the problem and how I solved it, the way you'd tell another developer. Keep the game name in, but no store link and no sales language. Under 200 words, with a title under 100 characters. End with a genuine question for readers. Also give me 3 short replies ready for likely comments: "what engine?", "isn't that too hard?", "where can I play it?" (that last one can include the link).
```

**Prompt 4: Challenge reply**

```
Someone beat my Echo ghost challenge. They got [their distance] m against my [my distance] m, and their username is [name]. Write: a short comment congratulating them, a caption for my repost of their result, and the on-screen hook for my rematch clip. Keep it playful and competitive, never sarcastic about them.
```

**Prompt 5: Weekly review**

```
Here are this week's numbers for my Echo clips. For each clip: platform, hook, pillar, views, average watch time, profile or link clicks, shares.
[paste table]
Play Console installs by source this week: [paste]
Day-1 return rate this week: [x]% (last week [y]%)

Tell me: which 2 or 3 clips earned the most clicks per view, which pillar works best on each platform, what to remix next week, what to drop, and whether any clip has earned a small paid test (only if day-1 return isn't falling). Be blunt.
```
